AFTERCARE · SYNTHETIC PATIENT DEMO

A conversation worth having.

You are playing Miguel Alvarez, age 71, three days after a knee replacement. Confirm that you are the demo participant and agree to continue. All symptoms are fictional. No telephone calls or emergency calls are placed by this demo.
01 · ENGLISH · WOUND CONCERN

When asked about your incision

“It is red and warm, and I had a fever last night.”

If asked about fever, confirm that you had a fever last night. For the other questions, say you have your prescriptions and understand them, have not fallen, are eating and drinking normally, and your daughter will drive you.

Watch: the patient moves to urgent review with the original quotation.
02 · ESPAÑOL · PREOCUPACIÓN POR LA HERIDA

Cuando le pregunten por la incisión

“Está roja y caliente, y tuve fiebre anoche.”

Confirme que tuvo fiebre anoche. Para las demás preguntas: recogió todas las recetas y entiende qué tomar, no se ha caído, come y bebe normalmente y su hija lo llevará a la cita.

Observe: aparece una alerta urgente con sus palabras originales.
03 · EMERGENCY INTERRUPT

Partway through the demo

“Actually, my chest hurts and I cannot breathe.”

En español: “Ahora me duele el pecho y no puedo respirar.”

Watch: routine questions stop, the fixed 911 instruction is issued, and an emergency alert remains open for the demo nurse. This is a fictional test; do not call 911 to rehearse.
04 · A PRACTICAL BARRIER

When asked about the appointment

“I do not have a ride to my appointment.”

En español: “No tengo transporte para mi cita.”

Watch: a same-day review task surfaces the transportation barrier. To ask for a person instead, say “Can I speak to a nurse?” / “¿Puedo hablar con una enfermera?”
